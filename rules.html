Park Rules & Regulations

These rules apply to all guests, residents, occupants, and visitors. Guests are responsible for ensuring their visitors follow park rules.

Open the original rules PDF (new tab)

1. Check-In / Check-Out

• Check-in: 1:00 PM • Check-out: 11:30 AM • Monthly residents are also subject to these park rules.

2. Quiet Hours

Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM. Excessive music, yelling, generators, engine revving, parties, or other disruptive noise is prohibited at all times.

3. Speed & Vehicles

• Park speed limit: 5 miles per hour • Drive carefully and watch for pedestrians, children, and pets. • Reckless driving and unnecessary vehicle noise are prohibited. • Vehicles must be parked only in approved areas and may not block roads, utilities, or neighboring sites.

4. Sites & Occupancy

Unless approved by management, each site is limited to: • Up to 3 adults • Up to 4 children • One RV • Two passenger vehicles • One accessory recreational vehicle and trailer, if space allows Only registered occupants may regularly live at a site. Visitors may not establish residency without management approval.

5. Site Appearance

Sites must remain clean, orderly, and free of excessive clutter. Outdoor furniture, grills, and normal camping equipment are allowed. Household furniture, appliances, trash, and excessive stored property may not accumulate outside.

6. Trash, Sewer & Water

• Place trash only in designated dumpsters. • Never dump gray water or black water on the ground. • Sewer connections must remain secure and leak-free. • Water must be used reasonably. • Report leaks or utility problems promptly.

7. Electrical Use

Do not overload, alter, or tamper with electrical pedestals. Unsafe electrical equipment is prohibited. Electric space heaters are prohibited due to electrical-system capacity.

8. Pets

Pets must be kept under control and leashed outside the RV unless otherwise permitted by applicable law. Owners must: • Clean up pet waste. • Prevent excessive barking. • Prevent aggressive behavior. • Prevent pets from roaming freely. Owners are responsible for damage or injury caused by their animals.

9. Children

Children must be appropriately supervised. Parents and guardians are responsible for their children's conduct and safety.

10. Fires & Grills

Campfires are permitted only in approved fire pits. Fires and grills must: • Be attended while in use. • Be kept away from combustible materials. • Comply with burn restrictions. • Be fully extinguished before leaving or sleeping.

11. RVs & Repairs

RVs must remain reasonably safe, clean, and operable. Major vehicle or RV repairs are prohibited. Minor or emergency repairs require management approval. Do not discharge oil, fuel, antifreeze, chemicals, or other fluids onto park property.

12. Conduct

Threats, fighting, harassment, intimidation, repeated unwanted confrontation, vandalism, illegal activity, or conduct that materially disturbs other guests is prohibited. Respect neighboring campsites and do not walk or drive through occupied sites without permission.

13. Firearms & Fireworks

Target shooting and discharge of firearms are prohibited except where legally justified in an emergency. Weapons may not be handled or displayed in a threatening, reckless, or unlawful manner. Fireworks require prior management approval.

14. Alcohol

Responsible alcohol use is permitted at individual sites. Public intoxication, fighting, disorderly conduct, or disturbances are prohibited.

15. Facilities & Common Areas

Park facilities are for registered guests and authorized visitors. Keep facilities clean and do not damage park property. Smoking is prohibited inside restrooms, showers, and other designated non-smoking facilities.

16. Laundry Facilities

Laundry facilities are for registered guests only and are used at the guest's own risk. Do not overload, misuse, or place items containing grease, fuel, chemicals, or excessive debris in machines. Remove laundry promptly when cycles end. Guests are responsible for damage caused by misuse. Adventure RV Park is not responsible for lost, stolen, damaged, or unattended clothing or personal property to the extent permitted by law.

17. Lighting

Exterior lights must not shine directly into neighboring RVs or campsites. Bright decorative or flood lighting should be turned off during quiet hours. Reasonable safety lighting is allowed.

18. Property & Liability

Guests are responsible for their RVs, vehicles, personal property, pets, visitors, and any damage they cause. Adventure RV Park is not an insurer of personal property and, to the extent permitted by law, is not responsible for losses caused by theft, weather, other guests, utility interruptions, or events outside the park's reasonable control.

19. Monthly Residents

• Rent is due on each resident's assigned monthly due date. • A $25 late fee applies on the 5th day after the due date. • An additional $7 per day applies beginning on the 6th day after the due date. • If rent remains unpaid by the 9th day after the due date, tenancy may be terminated by management, subject to applicable law. • At move-in, the full monthly rent of $425 is due regardless of the move-in date. • A $13-per-day credit will be applied for each day of the applicable rental period before the resident's move-in date. • Water, sewer, and electricity must be used reasonably. • Electric space heaters are prohibited. • Monthly residents must maintain comprehensive general liability insurance naming Adventure RV Park as an additional insured.

20. Rule Violations

Management may issue warnings or require correction of rule violations. Serious or repeated violations, including violence, dangerous conduct, illegal activity, major property damage, significant sanitation hazards, or repeated disturbances, may result in termination of occupancy as permitted by law.

21. Management Authority
